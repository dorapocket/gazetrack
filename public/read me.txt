Currently 7 sets are prepared in Photoshop, each with 5 images, named AB, A'B, AB', B, B'.
A = person, B = background, and ' indicates the difference. For example, A' means the difference is on the person.
Two more sets are still being edited, for a total of 9 sets.

Each participant will view all 9 sets, but the specific pairings are randomized:
- Three sets are AB vs A'B
- Three sets are AB vs AB'
- Three sets are B vs B'
Therefore, participants will not see a repeated image pair.
The order is fully randomized.

Make sure each pair is separated enough for comfortable viewing (eye tracking may be used, so screen size and viewing distance may need to be constrained to improve precision).
If possible, also record mouse trajectories.

Instruction text:
Welcome to this experiment. You will see two real-world scene images presented side by side. You need to find one difference between them within 60 seconds. You may use the mouse to assist your search. Left click on the object you select in either image. If it is incorrect, nothing happens; if it is correct, a green circle will appear and you will proceed to the next trial.
